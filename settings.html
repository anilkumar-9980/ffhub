Sensitivity & Controls

There is no universal “best” sensitivity. Start with a balanced preset, then change one value at a time.

SettingConservativeBalancedAggressive

How to test

In training, practice tracking a moving target, flicking to a fixed target, and controlling recoil at three distances. If you overshoot, reduce sensitivity slightly; if you cannot turn fast enough, increase it slightly.
